The problem

Set a fair standard time for a manual task. That meant accounting for how fast the operator actually worked and for the conditions she worked in, then adding allowances.

What I did

  • Broke the task into 6 elements, each with a description and clear end points.
  • Timed 20 cycles with a stopwatch.
  • Adjusted performance ratings for conditions, because low-contrast color markings and dim room lighting made two colors hard to tell apart, and for effort, because the operator kept trying to beat her own time.
  • Calculated normal time, then applied allowances: 5% personal, 8% fatigue for high concentration and monotonous, repetitive work done alone, and 0% delay since there was no machine time.
  • Built a BasicMOST analysis table in Excel.

Result

A standard time of 121 seconds, with 13% total allowances.
